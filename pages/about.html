THE WOMAN BEHIND THE BRAND

Meet Erika Elise, master makeup artist, licensed esthetician, educator, and founder of Erika Elise Beauty. With nearly two decades in beauty, Erika has built her artistry around a simple belief: when you look good, you feel good.

What began as a natural talent evolved into a business around 2007, followed by several years as a MAC makeup artist, becoming a licensed esthetician, and ultimately building a brand that brings together artistry, education, and thoughtfully curated beauty experiences.

Erika is known for natural full glam that makes a statement without overpowering the woman wearing it. Her approach is rooted in enhancement rather than transformation, combining skin-focused artistry, precise application, quality products, and an understanding of each client's individual features. Just as important as the finished look is the experience: elevated, approachable, and centered on making every woman feel seen and cared for.

Her natural gift for teaching inspired The Beauty Lab by Erika Elise, a hands-on experience where women can learn, experiment, ask questions, and understand the techniques and products behind the looks they love. The goal isn't a bigger makeup bag. It's a better understanding of what's inside it.

Grounded in faith and guided by purpose, Erika is continuing to shape Erika Elise Beauty into a brand that feels personal, intentional, and true to the woman experiencing it.

Because beauty isn't about becoming someone new. It's about bringing forward the most confident version of the woman who's already there.
